General Dermatology

Skin, hair and nail conditions. Information about symptoms, assessment and follow-up.

Acne Treatment

Acne is an inflammatory skin condition that occurs when the skin's sebaceous glands produce excess sebum and pores become clogged. It is commonly seen on the face, back, shoulders, and chest. Although it is prevalent during adolescence, it can also occur in adulthood and negatively affect a person's quality of life. Acne is not just an aesthetic problem but also an important dermatological condition affecting skin health.

Rosacea Treatment

Rosacea is a chronic skin condition characterized by redness, visible blood vessels, and inflammatory lesions mainly on the central face. It typically occurs in adults aged 30-50 and is more common in women. Rosacea is not only an aesthetic concern but also an important dermatological condition affecting skin health.

Hair Loss And Hair Treatments

Hair loss is a common condition that everyone experiences to some degree. Losing 50-100 hair strands per day is considered normal. However, increased shedding, thinning hair, or bald patches may indicate pathological hair loss. Hair loss is not only a cosmetic concern but can also signal underlying health problems.

Wart And Mole Treatment

Warts are contagious skin lesions caused by the Human Papilloma Virus (HPV), appearing as raised and rough structures on the skin. These viral infections can occur in various parts of the body and are important both aesthetically and medically. Because warts are contagious, they should be treated early.

Nail Diseases And Treatment

Nail diseases are disorders that affect the nail plate, nail bed, or surrounding skin, disrupting nail structure and appearance.

Skin Infections

Skin infections are conditions caused by microorganisms such as bacteria, fungi, or viruses that disrupt the skin’s natural structure and barrier function.

Fungal Infections

Fungal infections are common skin diseases caused by dermatophytes, yeasts, and molds. These infections develop in warm and moist areas of the body and manifest with various symptoms. Fungal infections can cause not only aesthetic problems but also itching, pain, and secondary infections. Early diagnosis and treatment are critical to prevent the spread of infection and reduce complications.

Allergy And Eczema Types

Allergy and eczema are chronic skin diseases characterized by dryness, itching, redness, and scaling of the skin. These conditions not only cause aesthetic problems but also significantly affect the quality of life. Allergy and eczema can occur in all age groups, and early diagnosis and treatment are important. In our clinic, modern and effective treatment methods are used for these conditions.

Urticaria

Urticaria is a skin condition characterized by sudden, itchy, red, and swollen lesions. These lesions usually disappear within a few hours and may reappear on different parts of the body. Urticaria can develop due to allergic reactions, infections, stress, or physical triggers. Although it can significantly affect quality of life, proper diagnosis and treatment can effectively control it.

Dermatitis

Dermatitis means inflammation of the skin and has different types. This condition manifests with itching, redness, scaling, and sometimes discharge. Dermatitis is not only an aesthetic problem but also a health issue that significantly impacts quality of life. Early diagnosis and treatment are critical to prevent progression and reduce complications.

Allergic Diseases

Allergic diseases are chronic health conditions that occur when the immune system overreacts to substances (allergens) that are normally harmless. These diseases not only affect the skin but also have significant impacts on the respiratory system, digestive system, and overall health. Allergic diseases can occur at any age, and early diagnosis and treatment are crucial. Our clinic employs modern and effective treatment methods for these conditions.

Vitiligo

Vitiligo causes pale or white patches when the skin loses its pigment-producing cells.

Alopecia

Alopecia is the general term for hair loss on the scalp or body and does not refer only to alopecia areata.

Keloid

A keloid is raised scar tissue that grows beyond the boundaries of the original wound.

Scabies

Scabies is a contagious skin infestation caused by the mite Sarcoptes scabiei.

Psoriasis

Psoriasis is a chronic, non-contagious skin disease associated with the immune system.

Herpes Simplex

Herpes simplex is an infection caused by HSV-1 or HSV-2 that can recur.

Shingles

Shingles develops when the varicella-zoster virus remaining in the body after chickenpox becomes active again.

Corns And Calluses

Corns and calluses are areas of skin that thicken and harden in response to repeated pressure or friction.

Atopic Dermatitis

Atopic dermatitis is a chronic, non-contagious form of eczema that can flare up over time.

Aesthetic Dermatology

The scope of dermatological procedures, suitability assessment and points to consider.

Botulinum Toxin Applications

Botox is a treatment that temporarily reduces the activity of facial muscles by injecting low and safe doses of botulinum toxin into the muscles. This softens the appearance of dynamic wrinkles and gives the face a more relaxed and youthful expression.

Filler Application

A minimally invasive procedure where hyaluronic acid and similar biocompatible substances are injected into the tissue to restore volume loss, enhance facial contours, and soften wrinkles.

Autologous Stem Cell Treatment

Autologous treatment is an autologous micrograft-based treatment approach aimed at tissue regeneration, focusing on strengthening hair roots in hair loss. Cellular contents obtained from the person's own tissue are applied to the scalp in a controlled manner.

PRP Platelet Rich Plasma Treatment

Platelet-rich plasma (PRP) is an autologous treatment method that uses the patient’s own blood, separated and concentrated by centrifugation, to stimulate natural healing and regeneration.

Mesotherapy Application

Mesotherapy is a targeted treatment method that delivers active ingredients such as vitamins, minerals, peptides, hyaluronic acid, and growth factors into the skin and scalp through microinjections.

Spot Treatment

Visible small vessel expansions around the face and nose; may be associated with rosacea, photoaging, and genetic predisposition.

Acne Scar Treatment

Acne scars encompass atrophic/hypertrophic marks and accompanying pigmentation changes that result from irregular repair of the dermal matrix during the healing phase after inflammatory acne. The type and depth of the scar determine the treatment plan.

Scar Treatment

Scars formed after trauma, surgery, burns, or inflammation can appear as flat, hypertrophic, or keloid. Tissue elasticity, color, and surface integrity are affected.

Liquid Facelift

Liquid facelift is a term for injectable approaches considered for facial volume and tissue support.

Medical Skin Care

A vacuum-assisted hydro-dermabrasion system providing simultaneous cleansing, peeling, extraction, and dermal infusion, offering a gentle medical skin care method for the skin barrier.

Thread Lifting

A minimally invasive method in which biocompatible threads such as PDO/PLLA/PLGA are placed in the dermal/subdermal planes to achieve tissue tightening through mechanical lifting and biostimulation.

Capillary Treatment

Visible small vessel expansions around the face and nose; may be associated with rosacea, photoaging, and genetic predisposition.

Lip Filler

Lip filler is an aesthetic procedure intended to give the lips a fuller, more defined and natural appearance.

Chin Filler

Chin filler is an aesthetic procedure intended to define the chin line and create a more balanced appearance among the facial features.

Cheek Filler

Cheek filler is an aesthetic procedure intended to define the cheekbones and give the facial features a more balanced appearance.

Nasolabial Fold Filler

Nasolabial fold filler is an aesthetic procedure intended to soften the appearance of the lines extending from the sides of the nose to the corners of the mouth.

Under-Eye Filler

Under-eye filler is an aesthetic treatment intended to give the eye area a brighter, more rested and refreshed appearance.

Nasal Filler

Nasal filler is an aesthetic procedure intended to adjust the shape of the nose without surgery and achieve a more balanced appearance.

Glabellar Filler

Glabellar filler is an aesthetic treatment considered to reduce the appearance of depressions between the eyebrows and achieve a more balanced facial expression.

Jawline Filler

Jawline filler is an aesthetic procedure intended to define the jawline and give the facial features a sharper, more balanced appearance.

Marionette Line Filler

Marionette line filler is an aesthetic procedure intended to soften the appearance of the lines extending from the corners of the mouth towards the chin.

Salmon DNA

Salmon DNA is a common name for PN or PDRN products containing fragments of fish-derived DNA.

Masseter Botulinum Toxin

Masseter botulinum toxin temporarily reduces activity in the masseter chewing muscle.

Botulinum Toxin For Excessive Sweating

Botulinum toxin can temporarily reduce nerve signals that stimulate sweat glands.

Laser And Energy-Based Methods

Understanding the differences between laser, light, radiofrequency and ultrasound methods.

BB Light

BB Light is a revolutionary technology in skin renewal and rejuvenation. This light-based system penetrates the deeper layers of the skin in a controlled manner, increasing collagen and elastin production. It provides highly effective results, especially in the treatment of rosacea and skin pigmentation. BB Light activates the skin’s natural healing mechanisms, resulting in a brighter, firmer, and healthier appearance.

Fractional Carbon Dioxide Laser

Ablative fractional laser creates microscopic thermal damage zones (MTZ) on the skin, enabling controlled remodeling and collagen stimulation.

Gold-Needle Radiofrequency

Using insulated microneedles, fractional RF energy is delivered into the dermis to stimulate collagen and elastin production, providing skin tightening and scar treatment benefits.

Medical Skin Care

A professional skincare protocol combining vacuum-assisted hydro-dermabrasion with simultaneous cleansing, peeling, extraction, and serum infusion.

Pico Second Laser

It is an advanced laser technology that creates a photomechanical effect with picosecond-scale pulse durations, micro-fragments pigment particles, and minimizes thermal damage in the surrounding tissue.

HIFU Non-Surgical Face Lift

High-Intensity Focused Ultrasound (HIFU) is a non-surgical tightening method that creates thermal coagulation points in the dermis and SMAS layer, promoting collagen remodeling.

Laser Hair Lightening

Laser hair lightening aims to reduce the visible colour of hair using laser energy.

Tattoo Removal

Laser tattoo removal aims to break down pigment particles within the skin.

Carbon Laser Peel

A carbon laser peel combines a carbon-containing topical product with treatment using specific laser systems.

Eyebrow Tattoo Removal

Eyebrow tattoo removal aims to reduce permanent makeup or microblading pigment with a laser.

Three-Dimensional Skin Analysis

Three-dimensional skin analysis supports assessment of the face and neck using digital images.
